Stopwise Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · applies to Stopwise: Day Planner & Route for Android

Stopwise has no account, no ads and no server of its own. Your plans are kept on your phone. To find places, draw maps and work out travel times, the app sends the places you search for and the points on your route to Google Maps Platform.

What stays on your phone

  • Your days and their stops, your errand list and your saved places.
  • Your home and your settings: how you usually get around, when you usually leave, reminders, language and theme.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage. The developer of Stopwise never receives it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

What is sent to Google

Stopwise uses Google Maps Platform to work. When you use these features, the following goes to Google:

  • Searching for a place: what you type, and a point near your home so that nearby results come first.
  • Maps and travel times: the locations of your home and stops, to draw the map and calculate walking, driving and transit routes, and to look up places' opening hours.
  • Your current location, only if you allow it: when you choose "use where I am" to set your home, and while the On the go screen is open, so the route can be re-planned from where you are. Stopwise does not use your location in the background.
  • Technical information that the Google Maps SDK collects itself, such as how the map is used and diagnostic data.

Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Platform terms. The developer of Stopwise does not receive it.

Opening other apps

When you open directions in another maps app (such as Google Maps, 2GIS or Yandex Maps), share a meet-up, or add it to your calendar, Stopwise passes the details to the app you choose. Only the meet-up's date, time and place are shared, never your home or the rest of your day. What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android's backup, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is made and stored by Google under Google's own privacy policy; the developer of Stopwise cannot see it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Location (optional): to set your home from where you are, and for On the go.
  • Notifications (optional): for reminders to leave and for errands that are due.
  • Exact alarms and run at startup: so reminders go off on time, and are set again after the phone restarts.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Children

Stopwise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
