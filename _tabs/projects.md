## Cup & Clock

![Cup & Clock: guided brew timers and a log of every bag and cup](/assets/img/projects/cup-and-clock.png){: w="1024" h="500" }

A coffee brewing companion for people who want a better cup at home. It guides you through each brew step by step, adjusts recipes to your cup size and taste, and keeps a log of your beans and every cup you rate.

- **Platform:** Android and iOS (coming soon)
- **Tech:** Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, Room, DataStore, Koin
- **Links:** [Privacy policy](/cup-and-clock/privacy/)

## Night Wick

![Night Wick: a sleep timer by candlelight](/assets/img/projects/night-wick.png){: w="1024" h="500" }

A sleep timer shaped like a candle. Hold to light it and the candle burns down while you drift off; over the last ten minutes it gently fades out whatever you're listening to, then pauses it. It also has a dim bedside candle for the nightstand and a wake alarm that starts quiet and rises.

- **Platform:** Android
- **Tech:** Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, Android foreground services and AlarmManager
- **Links:** [Privacy policy](/night-wick/privacy/)

## Stopwise

![Stopwise: your day, in the best order](/assets/img/projects/stopwise.png){: w="1024" h="500" }

A day planner for a day of stops. Add your appointments, errands and meet-ups, and Stopwise puts them in the best order, shows the route on a map with how long each trip takes, and tells you when to leave. It can suggest a closer branch of a pharmacy or bank, keep errands for a day when they fit your route, and re-plan from where you are once you're on the go.

- **Platform:** Android
- **Tech:** Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, SQLDelight, Google Maps Platform (Places, Routes, Maps SDK)
- **Links:** [Privacy policy](/stopwise/privacy/)
