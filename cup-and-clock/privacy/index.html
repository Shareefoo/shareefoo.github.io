Privacy Policy for Cup & Clock

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Cup & Clock ("the app") is a coffee brewing companion. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and we collect nothing.

What the app stores

The app saves the following on your device so it can work:

  • the bags of beans you add (name, roaster, origin, roast date, weight and how much is left)
  • the cups you brew and rate (brewer, amounts, time, your star rating, flavor tags and notes)
  • your favorite recipes and the changes you made to recipes
  • your settings, such as units, sound and vibration

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, send or sell any personal information.
  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.
  • The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses them on your phone to tell you when the next step of a brew starts and when a brew is done. These notifications are created on your device. No notification service of ours is involved.

Vibration and sound

The app can play a short sound and vibrate during a brew. This is controlled in Settings.

Backups

Your data is not backed up by the app itself. If your phone's own backup (such as iCloud or Google backup) is switched on, your phone may include the app's data in that backup under the rules of that service.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all the information it stored on your phone. You can also delete a single bag from its page, or a single cup from your brew log.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date at the top.
