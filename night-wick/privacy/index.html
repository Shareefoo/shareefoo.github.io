Night Wick Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · applies to Night Wick: Sleep Timer for Android

Night Wick does not collect, send or share any personal data. The app has no internet access, no account, no ads and no analytics. What it saves stays on your phone.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • Your settings: the timer length, fade-out, screen-off and haptics choices, and the bedside brightness.
  • Your wake alarm: its time, days, whether it is on, and the name of the sound you picked.
  • A short record of the nights you used the timer: the date, the timer length and which sounds were on. It is kept for a future feature that shows your nights, and it never leaves the phone.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android's backup, Android may include the app's settings in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is made and stored by Google under Google's own privacy policy; the developer of Night Wick cannot see it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Notifications: to show the running timer and the ringing alarm.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep the timer and the alarm working while the screen is off.
  • Exact alarms and run at startup: so the wake alarm rings on time, including after the phone restarts.
  • Full-screen notifications: so the alarm can open over the lock screen.
  • Vibration: for the alarm and the faint crackle as you hold the button.
  • Device admin (optional): only if you switch on "Turn screen off at the end". It is used for one thing, turning the screen off when the timer ends, and switching the setting off removes it.

Night Wick lowers and pauses audio playing in other apps when the timer ends. It does this through Android's media controls and does not read what is playing.

Children

Night Wick is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
